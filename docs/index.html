Beagle

Local voice in, local voice out — for macOS.

Named for, and dedicated to, Hugo — who wears the red thread.

Download for Apple Silicon Source on GitHub

macOS 14 or later · Apple Silicon · ~8 MB app, ~1 GB of models on first use

What he does

Dictate anywhere

Hold ⇧⌘L, talk, let go. The transcript is pasted at your cursor in whatever app you were already typing in — editor, browser, chat, terminal. His ears lift while he hears you, so you never have to wonder whether it is listening.

Read anything aloud

Select text and press ⇧⌘S, or drag a paragraph or a .txt file onto the floating orb.

Read the screen

⇧⌘R snaps a region of the screen, reads the text out of it, and speaks it. Useful for anything you cannot select.

Stay out of the way

A 44-point orb and a menu bar icon. No Dock tile, no window unless you ask for one.

Why he is quick

A single statically linked Swift binary — no Electron, no Python, no bundled runtime. Inference runs on the Apple Neural Engine rather than the GPU, which is what makes an always-on voice app practical instead of something that spins your fans.

Nothing leaves your Mac

Beagle downloads its two models once, then never talks to the network again. There is no account, no API key, no analytics, and no crash reporting.

Your microphone audio is transcribed on device and discarded. Nothing you dictate, select, or capture is stored anywhere but your own machine.

It is Apache-2.0 licensed and the whole thing is on GitHub, so you do not have to take any of that on faith.

What is next

A third mode: a local conversation companion you can think out loud with — the same two engines plus an on-device language model, with memory that stays on your Mac. Tracked in issue #8.
