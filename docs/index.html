What he does

Three ways to use your voice

Everything is a keystroke away. There is no window to open and no Dock icon — just a 44-point orb and a menu bar item.

Dictate anywhere

Hold ⌃⌥L, talk, let go. The transcript lands at your cursor in whatever app you were already typing in. His ears lift while he hears you, so you never wonder whether it is listening.

Read anything aloud

Select text and press ⌃⌥K, or drag a paragraph or a .txt file onto the orb. Speech starts after the first sentence rather than the last.

Read the screen

⌃⌥J snaps a region, pulls the text out of it with Apple Vision, and speaks it. For anything you cannot select.

Stay out of the way

Clicking the orb never steals focus from the app you are typing in — which matters, because the next thing it does is paste into that app.

Why he is quick

An 8 MB binary, not a browser

A single statically linked Swift binary. No Electron, no Python, no bundled runtime. Inference runs on the Neural Engine rather than the GPU, which is what makes an always-on voice app practical instead of something that spins your fans.

Privacy

Nothing leaves your Mac

The models download once. After that, Beagle works with the network switched off.

  • ✓No account, no API key, no sign-in

Install

Two commands

Drag it to Applications, then clear the quarantine flag — the build is signed ad-hoc rather than notarized.

# after dragging Beagle.app to /Applications
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Beagle.app

# or build it yourself
git clone https://github.com/loom-labs/beagle
cd beagle && ./Scripts/build-app.sh
